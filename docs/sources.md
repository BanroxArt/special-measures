## Notes on reading the sources

- **Effective-date extensions.** The June 30, 2025 orders against CIBanco, Intercam and Vector each said they were effective 21 days after publication. FinCEN extended all three to September 4, 2025 (90 FR 30826) and again to October 20, 2025 (90 FR 40974). The registry records both extensions and uses the last effective date.
- **CIBanco amendment.** 91 FR 20362 (April 16, 2026) allows certain transmittals needed for the Government of Mexico to liquidate CIBanco. The screener still reports a hit; the scope text says to read the amendment before blocking a liquidation payment.
- **MEXC Global.** Issued November 6, 2026 (FinCEN table; Treasury press release sb0685), published November 10, 2026 at 91 FR 72420, effective December 10, 2026. The order defines MEXC Global to include its branches, subsidiaries and offices outside the United States and successor entities, and excludes offices in the United States. Footnote 1 names MEXC Global Ltd / MX Global Ltd (Seychelles), MEXC Estonia OÜ, GIDA Technology AG (Switzerland), MXC Global Ltd (United Kingdom), and the formerly Singapore-incorporated MEXC Global Foundation Ltd and MEXC Singapore Pte Ltd. Those seven names are the registry's aliases, with `MEXC`, `MEXC Global` and `MXC Exchange` (the pre-2021 brand cited in the order). The only domain is `mexc.com`, from the user agreement the order cites. The USD 16.7 billion figure is the order's own assessment at 91 FR 72424; Treasury's press release says "at least $16 billion".
- **PM2BTC.** Issued September 26, 2024 (FinCEN table), published and effective October 11, 2024 at 89 FR 82499. The order defines PM2BTC as the collection of six services, each named by its domain: PM2BTC.ME, BTC2PM.ME, PM2CASHIN.ME, BTC2CASHIN.ME, PM2WM.ME and BTC2WM.ME. Those domains belong to the PM2BTC entry only; a test fails if any other entry lists them.
- **Pre-2022 final rules.** FBME (81 FR 18480, effective July 29, 2016), North Korea (81 FR 78715, effective December 9, 2016), Bank of Dandong (82 FR 51758, effective December 8, 2017) and Iran (84 FR 59302, published November 4, 2019, effective November 14, 2019) are cited to the first page of the Federal Register document. The eCFR source notes cite the page with the regulatory text instead.
- **Bitzlato.** The order notes it was previously known as ChangeBot.
- **Huione Group.** The final rule names Haowang Guarantee (formerly Huione Guarantee), Huione Pay PLC and Huione Crypto. The June 2026 proposal would add H-Pay Service PLC and successor entities; until that is final, H-Pay is a `watch` alias only.
- **A7 sub-agents.** The October 2026 proposal lists six businesses and "any other entity identified by FinCEN". Comments closed November 4, 2026.
- **Burma.** 31 CFR 1010.651 is in the current eCFR. The registry has not checked for administrative rulings affecting it.
