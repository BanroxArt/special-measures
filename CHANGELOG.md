# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-16)

- Registry of 20 FinCEN special-measure actions with dated, cited events, current to the eCFR issue of 2026-11-12 and the Federal Register through 2026-11-16, including the MEXC Global order effective 2026-12-10.
- Status on a date, with effective-date extensions and withdrawals.
- Counterparty screening by name, domain and jurisdiction with five dispositions.
- `watch` command that lists FinCEN special-measure documents the registry does not cite.
- Events carry an optional `issued` date from FinCEN's special measures table next to the Federal Register publication date. A final order is `pending` from its issue date and `in_force` only from its effective date.
- Federal Register publication and effective dates for the FBME, North Korea, Bank of Dandong and Iran final rules (Iran was previously dated on its effective date).
